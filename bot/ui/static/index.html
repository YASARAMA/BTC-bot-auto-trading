    How manual orders work

    The bot places a market order through the same executor it uses itself, so the fill, fees and history are recorded identically. In paper mode the fill is simulated; in live mode it is a real order.

    The automatic strategy keeps running. If it decides to sell (its own signal, your stop, or the take profit), it will close a position you opened by hand. Turn on the kill switch or stop the bot if you want full control.

    Turn the AI trader on

    The key is stored in .env next to the app and is only sent to Anthropic. Each decision costs a fraction of a cent to a few cents depending on the model. Changes apply the next time you press Start.

    What the AI can and cannot do

    It can: read the last candles and indicators, decide buy, sell or hold, propose a stop loss and take profit, and explain its reasoning in one sentence.

    It cannot: choose position size, bypass the daily loss limit, trade during a cooldown, or place an order while the kill switch is on. It sees no news and no data beyond the snapshot the bot sends it.

    How to read it
    
        
    Signals are computed on a closed candle and filled at the next candle's open. Stops and take-profits fill at their level, or at the open when a candle gaps through them. Fees and slippage from Settings are applied to every fill.
    
        
    Backtest results are hypothetical. Past performance does not predict future returns. A strategy that looks good on one period can lose on the next.
