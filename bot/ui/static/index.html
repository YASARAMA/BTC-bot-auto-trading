How to read it

        
Signals are computed on a closed candle and filled at the next candle's open. Stops and take-profits fill at their level, or at the open when a candle gaps through them. Fees and slippage from Settings are applied to every fill.

        
Backtest results are hypothetical. Past performance does not predict future returns. A strategy that looks good on one period can lose on the next.
